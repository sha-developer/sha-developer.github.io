Privacy Policy for Dino Draw: Kids Coloring Fun

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Dino Draw: Kids Coloring Fun ("the app") is developed by Wondertales ("we", "us"). The app is designed for children. This policy explains what information is handled when the app is used, and how.

Information we collect

We do not ask children for any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to share personal details with us or with other users.

Drawings

Pictures colored in the app are stored only on the device. When a grown-up taps Save, the picture is saved to the device's photo gallery. When a grown-up taps Share, the device's own share menu opens, and whatever is shared is handled by the app the grown-up chooses. We never receive these pictures.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. All ad requests are marked as directed to children under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as coming from users under the age of consent, and are limited to ads rated G. This means Google does not use data from the app for personalized or interest-based advertising, and the app does not use the Android Advertising ID.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the device type, operating system, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interactions. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Links outside the app

Any link that leaves the app, such as a link to our YouTube channel, is behind a parental gate that a young child is unlikely to pass.

Children's privacy

We comply with COPPA and applicable children's privacy laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
